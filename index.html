How to play

  1. Keep your mission secret. On your turn, reveal your private hand and objective only after the computer has been passed to you.
  2. Program the board. Select one instruction card, then choose any memory tile. You may move the staged card to another tile before running.
  3. Run the CPU. Your program counter advances one tile and executes its instruction. Empty tiles are NOP; branch instructions may continue execution, up to five instructions.
  4. Watch the registers. Instructions modify the shared R0–R3 values. Register values wrap between −8 and +7.
  5. Complete your objective. If the registers satisfy your secret objective after execution, you win. Otherwise, pass the computer to the next player.
INSTRUCTION KEY

MOVE sets · ADD / SUB changes · INC / DEC shifts by 1 · NEG reverses sign · CLR resets to 0 · SWAP exchanges registers · BRA moves your program counter
